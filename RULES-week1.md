# Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge — Week 1 rules

Source: https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week1-2026-10-05 (pulled 2026-10-07)

## Theme: "Touch Grass"
Build something with open-weight models or open-source AI that gets people off screens and
out into the world. Examples given: a bird-call identifier for trails, a garden planner keyed
to frost dates, a run-club route builder for fall foliage.

## Hard requirements
- Open-source AI is at the core of the project (open-weight models, open-source agent
  frameworks, or local inference).
- The write-up explains why open innovation matters for this particular build.
- **New project**, started and finished inside the challenge window (opens Oct 5).
- Commits made after the deadline have to be noted in the project README, or the entry
  can be disqualified.
- Published as a DEV post using the template on the challenge page, tagged
  `devchallenge` and `hf26challenge`.
- **Deadline: 2026-10-11, 11:59 PM PDT.** Winners are announced the week of Oct 12.
- Entrants must be 18 or older. Teams of up to 4 make one submission between them; one
  member publishes it and credits the rest in the post.
- AI use: "Use of AI is allowed as long as all other rules are followed."
- Optional: save the agent session with DevRelay and embed it in the post with the
  `agent_session` liquid tag, or link to it.

## Judging (writing is weighted most)
1. Writing quality
2. Relevance to the prompt and theme
3. Creativity
4. Technical execution
5. Use of partner technology (optional bonus)

## Prizes
- Overall winner: $250, DEV++ membership and a badge.
- Featured partners, $200 each: **Gemma** (Google's open-weight model, run locally,
  fine-tuned, or served through Google Cloud), **DigitalOcean** (GPU Droplets, Gradient AI),
  Arduino (UNO Q), Render, TabPFN, Tinker (fine-tune with a documented improvement).
- Standard partners, $100 each, e.g. Backboard. The page lists 16 partner categories in all.
  No AMD or Ollama category was found.
- Partner credits: hacktoberfest.com/my/promos
